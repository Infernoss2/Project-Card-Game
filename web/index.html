    How to play

    • Everyone gets 3 face-down cards and 6 cards in hand. Pick 3 of your hand cards to lay face-up on top of the face-down ones.
    • On your turn, play a card equal or higher than the top of the pile. You can play several cards of the same value at once.
    • Keep 3 cards in hand while the deck lasts. When your hand is empty, play your face-up cards, then your face-down cards blind.
    • Can't play? You pick up the whole pile.

    Special cards

    • 2 Play on anything. Resets the pile.
    • 3 Play on anything. Invisible: the next player beats the card under it.
    • 7 Next card must be 7 or lower.
    • 8 Skips the next player.
    • 10 Play on anything. Burns the pile and you go again.
    • Q Blocks 2, 3 and 10.
    • 4 of a kind in a row burns the pile.
